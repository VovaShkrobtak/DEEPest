# Environment model v1 — first playable

Implemented: pure RefCounted owner of weather and three persistent, well mixed room compartments. No UI or clock dependency. State is JSON-compatible dictionaries, seconds, kelvin, kg and joules. `initial_state(seed_value=1998)`, `advance(state,dt,context)`, `apply(state,effect)`, `conditions(state,location)`, `observe(state,location)` are the public contract. Advance returns `{type,text}` events when fires go out. Context is read-only: `heating_water: bool` and `location: String` request a water heat export from the matching room.

State schema: `seed`, `time_s`, `weather {temp_k,wind_m_s,rain}`, `rooms {shelter,clinic,workshop}`. Each room holds `temp_k`, aerosol `smoke`, O2 volume fraction `oxygen`, `window_open`, `sealed`, `chimney_clean`, `fuel_kg`, `burning`, `ash_kg`, `burned_kg`, `exhaust_wood_kg`, `last_water_w` (last substep export power), `water_transfer_j` (cumulative exported heat), `energy_j` (cumulative chemical release), `flue_energy_j`, `heat_loss_j` (signed net environment exchange). Initial rooms are cold (6 °C), have no loaded fuel and dirty chimneys. Kernel transfers inventory fuel before `add_fuel`; the environment never creates or removes inventory fuel itself.

Effects: `clean_chimney`, `light_fire`, `add_fuel {mass_kg}`, `extinguish`, `ventilate` (toggle), `seal_window`; optional `location` defaults to shelter. Invalid locations, nonpositive/nonfinite fuel and nonpositive/nonfinite elapsed time have no effect. Sealing reduces infiltration but leaves a deliberately open window open. Moving away does not stop room updates. Outdoor locations all use regional conditions.

## Assumptions and numerical method

All coefficients below are uncalibrated prototype assumptions, not measured reference data or a full implementation of the specification. Model validity is qualitative cold-season indoor survival; there is no clinical prediction.

Weather is an analytic function of absolute simulated day and seed: smooth daily, 365-day seasonal, and multiday sine components. No frame RNG or frame-dependent weather. Seasonal mean starts in autumn and trends toward winter. Rain is a bounded 0–1 intensity. Wind remains 0.5–3.5 m/s. There are no snowpack, radiation, pressure or spatial fronts yet.

Rooms combine air, walls and furniture into effective heat capacity C=900 kJ/K. Heat transfer uses the exact exponential solution of C dT/dt = useful combustion power − H(T−Tout), with midpoint weather held over each <=10 s substep. H comprises envelope 30/48 W/K sealed/unsealed plus air exchange. Air inventory is represented by 36 kg effective inventory; infiltration 0.00008/0.0002 s⁻¹, open window 0.003 + 0.0003 wind s⁻¹. Smoke and oxygen use analytic well-mixed source/exchange solutions. Tests compare 10/60 s calls; fractional call boundaries may produce small midpoint errors.

Wood burns at <=0.00012 kg/s, limited by remaining fuel and oxygen-dependent kinetic rate. Lower heating value 15 MJ/kg, 55% retained room heat, 45% flue heat. Burned wood is partitioned as 1% ash and 99% wood-derived gas/exhaust, with oxygen exchange represented separately in the atmospheric reduction. No full chemical species closure, moisture or ignition/pyrolysis phases. `fuel + burned` is conserved without external additions; `burned = ash + exhaust_wood`. Chemical energy equals retained thermal increment plus signed heat exchange plus flue loss plus exported water heat. When the kernel requests `heating_water` at a matching location, `min(useful combustion heat, 900 W × dt)` is exported to the water model and deducted before room heating. `last_water_w` resets each substep, including when the stove is out. Kernel must only request this sink when an actual water vessel is being heated, then pass this power to the items domain. The kernel uses 10-second steps; `last_water_w` is the final-substep power for larger external calls, so a larger cross-domain integration must consume the cumulative transfer delta instead. No additional drying heat source is created: clothing drying uses room conditions. Oxygen sink is 1.4 kg/kg wood normalized by effective air inventory; this is an aggregate approximation rather than gas-species chemistry.

Smoke is an explicitly dimensionless aerosol proxy, not a measured CO concentration: dirty chimney source 2.5/kg wood, clean chimney 0.04/kg. Ventilation removes it exponentially while increasing heat loss. No CO sensor readings or hidden oxygen numbers reach observation. Physiology consumes the proxy through the kernel contract. Extinguishing stops new sources but leaves room smoke and heat persistent. Fire spread, CO and toxic-dose modeling remain specified.

Observations expose qualitative temperature, weather, visible stove/window state, soot and perceptible smoke warnings. Only shelter has an assumed wall thermometer, displaying rounded room °C. Exact oxygen and smoke remain internal.

## Verification

`godot --headless --path . --script tests/test_environment.gd` runs standalone SceneTree assertions: finite 100-day weather, depletion and nonnegative fuel, ash/exhaust wood mass ledger, chemical/thermal energy ledger, smoke accumulation/O2 reduction, ventilation recovery, open-window heat loss, invalid-input rejection, 10/60-second call equivalence, water-export energy closure, lower room heating under kettle load, no export from an unlit stove or another room. These are property checks of the stated assumptions, not experimental calibration. Save/replay and cross-domain physiology acceptance are kernel integration responsibilities. Complexity O(rooms × elapsed/10 seconds); no wall-clock or per-frame update.
