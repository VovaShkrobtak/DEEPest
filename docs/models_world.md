# World model v1

`src/domain/world.gd` is a UI independent RefCounted model with dictionary save state. `initial_state(seed_value)`, `advance(state, dt_s, context)`, `apply(state,effect)`, `availability(state,effect)`, `observe(state, location)`, `locations()` form its public contract. Application code owns commands, local access, player inventory and atomic exchanges. World owns its stocks and NPC inventories; application must check both sides before transfer. `availability` checks finite resource limits and NPC proximity when effect includes player `location`. Application must supply that location.

The seven fixed places form a star around the yard. Each connection stores metres. NPC speed is an assumed 1 m/s, arrival rounded up to a minute. Shared physiology handles exertion, hunger energy, temperature, sleep and hydration; Nina starts with a small cut which actual bandaging reduces. NPCs start with known routes and containers of 2 kg water capacity. Their narrow policy consumes stocked water/food, fetches water below 0.5 kg, and returns home. They traverse each edge without teleporting; arrival and drawing water produce location-tagged facts. Death stops decisions and intake. No GOAP, rumours, autonomous food production, combat or general road replanning is claimed.

World uses persisted `pending_s` and fixed minute ticks. Identical ordered context values and action boundaries produce identical state for minute steps and bulk advancement. Subminute residuals are deferred (maximum 59.999 s); weather changing within that residual is sampled at next minute. Cost is proportional to simulated minutes × 2 NPCs, not rendering frames. Exact weather integration and a long-term scheduler remain specified.

Initial well reserve is 1000 kg without replenishment; wood and fauna reserves are deterministic seeded amounts. Removal never increases them and revisiting does not regenerate stock. NPCs compete sequentially in stable dictionary insertion order; taking the remaining water prevents the second NPC duplicating it. Consumed NPC water and food have cumulative flow counters, and physiology records body losses. Food unit is one ration, assumed 2.8 MJ; water intake 2 kg/day and ration intake 3/day are prototype assumptions, not calibrated medical needs. Actual body losses depend on the shared physiology model. Barter transfers one player ration to NPC stock for two NPC cloth pieces; repeated exchange exhausts cloth. Trust is bounded and only changes for direct encounters, with 32 retained direct memories. Small talk increment is a prototype social assumption, not a psychological claim.

Garden stores seeds, soil water in kg and plant biomass in kg. One seed starts a two kg fresh crop over 30 suitable days (temperature above 5°C, soil water above 0.1 kg and daylight 06:00–18:00). This is an uncalibrated aggregate boundary model: half the new fresh biomass comes from stored soil water, half from an explicitly counted atmosphere/soil photosynthesis boundary (`photosynthesis_input_kg`); water transfer is counted in `plant_water_kg`. Rain input uses dimensionless weather intensity 0–1 with an assumed 0.2 kg/min at full intensity; fixed evaporation is 0.002 kg/minute and is accounted in a sink counter. No soil nutrition, transpiration, seasons, disease, fauna reproduction or food-chain interaction is implemented. Hunting removes finite biomass and does not recreate it. Harvest requires mature growth and removes actual biomass; application supplies the resulting inventory transfer.

Observation only includes present stationary NPCs and qualitative local resources. It does not expose reserve mass, trust values, physiological truth or offscreen travel. Application filters location-tagged events before displaying them. Save data contains the complete model, including routes, NPC bodies, residual time and memory.

Validation: `godot --headless --path . --script tests/test_world.gd` checks seed replay, NPC water withdrawal, no offscreen observation, minute/bulk equivalence, reserve depletion, remote trade rejection and finite barter. Coefficients are assumptions; none have experimental calibration. Full target acceptance E01–E08 remains partially specified; tests cover only this documented slice.

Environmental context uses `weather.temp_k`, `wind_m_s`, `rain` and read-only `rooms[location]` (`temp_k`, `smoke`, `oxygen`) for stationary indoor NPCs. Room pollution affects the same physiology as player pollution. `context.hour` is current hour if supplied; fallback starts day zero at 16:20 plus simulated time. Dead or unconscious NPCs refuse social exchanges. Crop reseeding starts only after actual harvest cleared biomass; a second harvest is rejected.
