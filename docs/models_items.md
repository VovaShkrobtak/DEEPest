# Items model, schema 1

Implemented by `src/domain/items.gd`, pure RefCounted. State is saveable dictionaries, with no external clocks or random numbers. `initial_state`, `validate`, `apply`, `advance`, `observe`, `mass_kg`, `capacity_kg` are public. Effects use `kind` (`type` and `action` aliases accepted). Invalid effects including NaN, noninteger stacks, unknown IDs and exceeding capacity leave state unchanged, including if `apply` is called directly.

Stack IDs: wood, cloth, bandage, ration, matches, wire, parts, seed, tool, potato, cooked_potato. Quantities are counts; per-unit masses are kg in `MASS`. Water `volume_kg` is mass (density assumed 1 kg/l for presentation), capacity 2 kg. `microbes` and `chemical` are normalized concentration per kg, not diagnoses. Mixing conserves mass, solute burden and water thermal energy. Water history is qualitative and observations never expose hidden contaminants. Initial carried kettle mass is 0.5 kg. Repair materials move into `installed_mass_kg`, preserving total carried mass; repairs consume work capacity `tool_wear_j`. Bandage conversion preserves cloth mass. Dropping/eating/removing transfers inventory out of this owner's boundary; root must account for receiving body, fire, ground, or NPC.

Effects: take_water(mass_kg,temp_k,microbes,chemical), remove_water(mass_kg), add_item/remove_item/eat/drop(item,amount), craft_bandage, repair_radio, repair_cart, switch_radio, switch_lamp, boil, cook. Boil does not instantly change water. Root owns action time, location, active stove preconditions, and supplies `heating_water` and `fire_w` while heating. Cook converts one potato to one cooked potato after root's timed heating action; no universal disinfection or spoilage reset is asserted. Root must validate recipe resources again on completion. Repeat repair fails.

Water lump heat capacity is m*4180 + 250 J/K, Newton cooling 1.5 W/K and at most 1000 W delivered stove heat; integration uses exponential steps at most 10 s and cap at 373.15 K. Closed kettle retains all water; excess power at boiling is rejected heat, not accumulated energy. Near-boiling exposure >=372.15 K causes microbial concentration exponential decay with assumed 10 s characteristic time; 60 s exposure changes observable treatment history. Chemical concentration never changes under heating. Water added later resets exposure. 2 kg from room temperature requires approximately 13–15 min of sustained stove heating, followed by exposure. This is an intentionally limited, uncalibrated gameplay assumption: no actual pathogen safety guarantee, freezing/evaporation/altitude/container fouling model yet.

Electricity is an ideal finite 18000 J battery, lamp 3 W and repaired radio 1 W switched parallel loads. Energy drains exactly by load*time, switches open at depletion, no recharge. Circuit voltage, wire resistance, waste heat and signal strength are still specified, not implemented. Radio must be repaired before switching on. Carry limit is 18 kg or 45 kg with repaired cart; geometry, hands, route restrictions, rolling resistance and cart wear remain specified. Caller physiology uses total load; cart is not a force multiplier within physiology itself.

Food `food_age_warm_s` is cumulative warm-equivalent exposure: dt*2^((T-293.15)/10), bounded between 1/16 and 16 times. Odour becomes observable after 172800 warm-equivalent seconds. This is one batch aggregate and an assumption, not a calibrated spoilage or toxin model. Cooking does not reset it. Initial long-life rations share this conservative aggregate with potatoes; separate batch histories remain specified.

Verification: `tests/test_items.gd` checks failed-transaction immutability, cloth conversion, repair idempotency and mass balance, finite circuit charge, liquid mixing, delayed heating with chemical retention, finite capacity, cart repair and temperature-dependent food ageing. Run with Godot 4.6.3 headless; no Android hardware profiling or experimental calibration claimed.
